Case study 01 / Nutanix

Scaling enterprise migration at Nutanix.

NutanixBangalore2022–2026Member of Technical Staff → Team Lead, Engineering

Parag combined engineering delivery, customer discovery, people leadership, and commercial alignment to scale migration products serving enterprise customers with highly complex data and infrastructure environments.

01 / Context

Infrastructure at enterprise scale.

Enterprise customers held more than 10 petabytes of data. Migration work required coordination across engineering, product, sales, customer onboarding, testing, legal, and compliance.

Long testing cycles had become a bottleneck to shipping software. The work was as much about building a capability and operating rhythm as it was about writing code.

02 / Building the capability

From a product feature to a repeatable capability.

  • Led development of a file-migration capability and coordinated enterprise customer onboarding across organisational teams.
  • Helped generate more than $1M in recurring revenue through the capability and onboarding work.
  • Worked on SQL-backed product systems and established coding standards and review practices that reduced bugs found in code by 20%.

03 / Team and roadmap

Connecting customers to the roadmap.

Parag partnered with Sales to identify customer needs and prioritise features, aligning Sales, Product, and Engineering around customer-driven priorities. He developed and presented a six-month roadmap with a VP, including its customer-growth and ACV case.

He supported two engineers’ promotions through regular feedback, clear goals, measurable expectations, and exposure to senior leadership. A workflow redesigned with AI tooling and Jira reduced product cycles by 20% and meetings by 50%.

04 / Risk and customers

More capacity. Less exposure.

Parag identified testing duration as a release bottleneck, secured additional computing capacity and testing resources, and increased delivery from two to three major releases annually, in addition to interim releases.

With Legal and Compliance, he conducted technical diligence and remediation on Nutanix Move. Critical security risks reduced from more than 400 to 20; licensing risks reduced from more than 500 to zero.

He also helped banking customers move workloads from public cloud environments back to on-premises infrastructure, supporting a migration involving 20,000 workloads for an anonymous global custodian bank.

05 / Leadership approach

Parag’s leadership style combines clear ownership, customer context, measurable goals, frequent feedback, and opportunities for team members to gain visibility.
