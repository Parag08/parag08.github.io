Scaling enterprise migration

Making a migration capability trustworthy enough for regulated, petabyte-scale customers — and turning it into a repeatable program across Sales, Product, Engineering, and customer teams.

Context

Move lifts virtual machines off VMware, Hyper-V or AWS and onto the Nutanix Cloud Platform with minimal downtime — configurations preserved, no refactoring required. The commercial problem was not “can we migrate a VM.” It was whether a customer with petabytes of data and regulated workloads would trust the path.

My scope

I led the files migration feature and the cross-organisational work to onboard customers with 10+ petabytes of data, generating $1M+ in recurring revenue. Later I led the 11+ engineer team and its $500K budget, owned technical due diligence, and aligned Sales, Product, and Engineering on deals that contributed to $40–50M in new-business ACV.

Approach

The work ran as one program: product requirements, engineering delivery, security diligence, and customer onboarding were coordinated rather than managed as separate workstreams. That mattered for banking customers moving from public cloud back on-premises, including a global custodian bank shifting 20,000 workloads.

Technical due diligence sat alongside delivery. It reduced 400+ critical security risks to 20 and 500+ licensing risks to zero, while delivery cycle time shortened by about 20%.
