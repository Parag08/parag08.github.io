EverHaus · Singapore · 2026

Designing an AI-enabled investment platform

EverHaus needed a product strategy that could survive regulation, data architecture, and a lean operating model — not a feature list dressed as a vision. I translated market research and compliance constraints into a buildable platform.

Twenty-plus features were defined; fifteen were built. A six-month plan compressed to two months by being explicit about what the first operating version had to prove, and what could wait.
