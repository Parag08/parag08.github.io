EverHaus · Singapore · 2026 · Product strategy

Designing an AI-enabled investment platform

Defining a product strategy that could survive regulation, data architecture, and a lean operating model — then compressing a six-month plan into two.

Context

EverHaus needed a platform strategy grounded in market research, compliance requirements, and the realities of a lean operating team. I translated those constraints into a buildable feature set.

My scope

I defined the product strategy, translated market research and compliance requirements into a buildable feature set, and shaped the operating model around a lean team.

Approach

Twenty-plus features were defined against the operating model. Fifteen were built. The plan compressed from six months to two by being explicit about what the first operating version had to prove, and what could wait.
