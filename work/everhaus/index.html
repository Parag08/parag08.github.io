Case study 03 / EverHaus

Designing an AI-enabled investment platform.

EverHausSingapore2026Product strategy

Working with a bootstrapped founding team, Parag translated market research, user needs, and Singapore compliance requirements into a practical product roadmap and operating model.

01 / Understand

Start with the market, not the feature list.

Parag brought together market research and user needs to define an investment product that could be understood, operated, and improved by a lean founding team.

He defined more than 20 features and worked with the founders to validate which problems were worth solving first.

02 / Translate

Turn compliance into product decisions.

Singapore MAS requirements were translated into practical product and operating decisions rather than treated as a separate legal layer. This created a clearer path from customer promise to implementation.

The case study focuses on product strategy and translation work; it does not claim regulatory approval or investment performance.

03 / Build

A roadmap a small team could actually ship.

Fifteen features were built and founder-validated. The operating model and setup sequence reduced the expected setup timeline from six months to two months.

04 / Operating design

Strategy is useful when it changes what happens next.

The result was a practical roadmap and lean operating design that connected customer insight, compliance, delivery capacity, and the founding team’s immediate decisions.
