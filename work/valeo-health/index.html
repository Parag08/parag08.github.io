Valeo Health · UAE · 2026 · Product management & operations

From rising fleet costs to an adopted routing product

Turning an invisible fleet-cost problem into a routing product the operations team adopted in its first week.

Context

The operations team was absorbing distance, fuel, and time they could not see clearly. I started with the financial diagnosis, then sat with the people running the routes, and specified a product around what they were actually missing.

My scope

I led the financial diagnosis, worked with the operations team on the route-level decisions that mattered, specified the routing application, and built the Valeo Smart Tracker prototype.

Approach

The cost baseline came first, so the problem had a number rather than only a narrative. The product was then designed around frontline adoption: the workflow had to fit the people running the routes, because a routing tool nobody used would not remove a single kilometre.

Separately, I specified and built Valeo Smart Tracker — a working prototype for medication and health-habit adherence, with a daily care plan, refill alerts, and streaks that motivate without punishing a missed day.
