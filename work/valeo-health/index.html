Case study 02 / Valeo Health

From rising fleet costs to an adopted routing product.

Valeo HealthUAEManagement InternJuly–August 2026

When fleet operating costs increased 18% while revenue remained flat, Parag combined financial analysis, operations research, and AI-assisted product development to identify the causes and implement a practical solution.

01 / Diagnose

Find the cost inside the cost.

Parag analysed financial and operating data using Cursor with Claude, investigating why fleet costs had risen by 18% while revenue stayed flat. The analysis identified the primary drivers of the increase and created a basis for action.

02 / Design the strategy

Make the opportunity legible.

He developed two fleet-cost strategies, evaluated consolidation of two operating verticals, and designed a three-hub network intended to reduce distance travelled per order.

Up to 30% broader fleet savings was a proposed strategic opportunity, not a realised result.

03 / Build with users

A product shaped by the people using it.

Parag specified and prototyped a route-optimisation application directly with the operations team. AI-assisted development helped build the application quickly while direct frontline input kept the workflow practical.

04 / Secure adoption

Approval is part of the product.

Parag presented the financial and operating strategies to the CEO and operations leadership, explaining expected KPI and return improvements at scale. Leadership approval was secured, followed by 100% operations-team adoption within one week.
