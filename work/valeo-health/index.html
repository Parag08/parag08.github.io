Slot 03 · Valeo Health · Dubai, UAE · Jul – Aug 2026 · Management intern, product and operations

From fleet costs to an adopted routing product

Fleet cost had risen 18% with no more orders served. I traced it from the receipts, won CEO approval for three strategies, then built Logistics Valeo myself and worked alongside operations until it stuck. Orders captured rose to 80–90%, and km per order fell about 25%.

Mission

The problem

Valeo Health sends 10–12 nurse–driver pairs to patients’ homes for short visits: sample collection, IV therapy and injections, 50–70 orders a day. Orders arrive on a partner booking platform and go to whichever provider claims them first.

Dispatch claimed orders by hand in Excel and coordinated the ground team at the same time, so many orders were missed. Between visits, idle pairs waited at the office, so the fleet drove long, empty kilometres that nobody could see clearly.

Strategies unlocked

Three plans the CEO approved

  • Built and liveThree hubs, aligned incentives and a digital app

    A 3-hub standby model. The operations team earns AED 50 a day when the team’s km per order is 35–45, and AED 100 below 35. Logistics Valeo runs all sample-collection and IV orders.

  • Approved planCombine babysitting and home nursing

    Share one fleet and one set of operations managers across both businesses.

  • Approved planA digital app for both businesses

    Extend the same digital operating model to babysitting and home nursing.

Patch notes

Every objection got an answer

Operations pushed back on every change. Each objection was answered with data or with a shipped change, usually within days.

  1. “Why use a tool when the current setup works?”

    ProofIt didn’t work: orders were being missed, and dispatch barely had time to claim orders and coordinate the ground team. I showed them the missed orders.

  2. “The third hub is barely used; my orders are around hub 2.”

    ProofTrue on some days, but the 3-hub model saves money overall. It was fitted on 9 months of historical orders, and I walked them through the data.

  3. “I get all the morning orders while other nurses sit idle.”

    PatchFair. I changed the roster algorithm to spread morning shifts evenly.

  4. “We have too many split shifts.”

    PatchThe roster now allows at most two split shifts.

  5. Pushback on the auto-accept lead time

    PatchLead time moved from 3 to 24 hours, and night hours stopped counting.
